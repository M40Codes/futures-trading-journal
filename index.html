How The Estimate Works

Each active condition contributes a bull probability and a weight. The model converts that bull probability into log-odds, multiplies by the weight, sums the active signals, and converts the result back into a long/short probability.

This is decision support, not a guaranteed true probability, because many market variables are correlated. Best use: repeatable prep dashboard, then validate with your own journal and sample size.
